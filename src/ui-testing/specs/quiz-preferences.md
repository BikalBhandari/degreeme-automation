# Spec: Quiz — Preferences

## Description
The final quiz step asks what the user would enjoy in their work. This is optional — a "Skip to results" button advances to the results page.

## Preconditions
- User completed or skipped the Environments step

## Screen Elements
- Subheading: "Preferences"
- Question: "What do you think you'd enjoy in your work?"
- Instruction: "Select all that apply:"
- Options (multi-select):
  - Designing or managing projects
  - Solving complex problems with data
  - Collaborating with others
  - Working with diverse communities
  - Teaching or mentoring others
  - Working at the intersection of tech and people
  - Exploring global or political systems
  - Helping others in crisis or recovery situations
  - Improving education through new technology
  - Designing sustainable or nature-inspired solutions
  - Managing nonprofit or mission-driven organizations
  - Communicating complex ideas across diverse audiences
- Navigation: "Back" link, "Skip to results" button

## Test Steps
1. Navigate to Preferences screen
2. Verify all 12 preference options are visible
3. Select multiple options
4. Click "Skip to results" — advances to results
5. Click "Back" — returns to Environments

## Expected Result
- All preference options are displayed and selectable
- "Skip to results" advances to the results page
- Back returns to Environments
